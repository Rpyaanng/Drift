## New

- **Chat over everything you watch.** Messages from your hub now show over its Surf stream and over a broadcast you're watching, not just a video you watch together, on the monitor that's showing it. Press Enter over a broadcast to answer without opening Together.
- **Chat over your games.** While you broadcast, your hub's messages show in the corner of what you're showing, on top of games too (set them to borderless or windowed). Viewers don't see them in the stream.
- **A nudge to share your cursor.** When you start watching a broadcast, its bar stays out with a note pointing at "Share my cursor", and goes once you turn it on.

## Fixed

- **Messages from friends in the voice call show over the video again.** Chat over the video now shows every message by default; if yours was set to "From people who can't be heard", it switches over once. That choice is still in Together → Options, and now only counts someone as heard when you're actually connected to them in the call.
- 4K HDR videos no longer freeze while a friend watches along: conversion tone maps on the graphics card first, so your copy and theirs both keep up.
