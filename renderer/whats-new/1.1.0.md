## New

- **3D geometry scenes** (Scenes tab, third row): Platonic Solids, Wire Globe (with a ring equaliser), Hex Tunnel, Cube City (a skyline of equaliser columns), Lattice and Menger Sponge. Lattice and Menger Sponge are the heaviest; if they stutter, set **Render quality** to **Light** in Settings.
- **Your own colours**: the pencil swatch at the end of the Colour row opens four colour pickers (background, shadows, main colour, highlights). **Match to main colour** fills in the other three for you.
- **Spotify volume**: while Spotify is the music source, the volume bar sets Spotify's own volume (its slot in the Windows volume mixer). Before Spotify has played anything it has no slot, so the bar sets the PC's main volume instead; hover over the bar to see which one it's controlling.
