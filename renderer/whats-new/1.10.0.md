## New

- **Blackjack, in the Games room.** Pick **Blackjack** under **Scenes → Games room** and you're at a casino table: hit, stand, double, split, insurance and surrender. The dealer stands on all 17s and blackjack pays 3 to 2. Bets are 10 to 500, and you start with 1,000 chips. The automatic scene changes never pick it, and never take you away from it mid-hand.
- **Play side by side with your hub.** Online in a hub, you sit at the hub's table with everyone there, up to six at once, each playing the dealer. You see each other's cards, pointers and profile pictures. Click someone at the table in the hub bar to sit down next to them. Alone, the table is all yours, and your chips are kept for next time.
- **Biggest pots.** Each hub keeps a board of its five biggest wins, one each, and it stays between sessions. A new record lights up.
- **Out of chips?** You're out for 20 seconds, then back in with 1,000. At a hub, everyone starts with 1,000 each time they sit down.
- **Stand up** once your hand is played to watch the table, and **Sit down** again when you like. If the table is full, you wait in line for the next seat.
- **Lit by your palette.** A lamp in your accent colour hangs over the table, the felt and the rail take their colours from your palette, and the light warms when you win. The felt carries Drift's own mark, and so do the cards' backs.
- **Table sounds.** Cards, chips, your turn, and how your hand went. They have their own switch, **Blackjack table sounds** in the Hub tab's Sounds, so they play even with the hub's sounds off.
