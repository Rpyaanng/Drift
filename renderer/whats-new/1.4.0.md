## New

- **Autoplay** (Video tab, next to Sound). On, the next video in the folder plays when one ends, as before. Off, the video stops on its last frame and pressing play starts it again from the beginning.
- **Close this monitor.** The "Close Drift?" prompt has a new button that turns Drift off on that one monitor and keeps it running on the others. Turn the monitor back on in **Settings → Monitors**. The button only shows when more than one monitor is on.

## Changed

- **One Span / Mirror / Mix setting.** It now lives in **Settings → Across monitors** and covers scenes and videos alike. The Video tab's separate "Mirror on every screen / Span across all screens" choice is gone: before, a video only spanned when both settings said Span. The Scenes tab shows the current choice and opens Settings when clicked. Your current Span / Mirror / Mix choice is kept.
- **Joining a video in progress.** A monitor that switches to the video already playing on another monitor now joins it at the same point, instead of starting it over for everyone.
