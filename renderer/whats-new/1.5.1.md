## New

- **Videos carry on where you left them.** Drift remembers how far you got in each video, even after you close it, and the next time you open one it picks up from there. The video that was playing when you closed Drift carries on from its spot too.
- **Watched bars.** Video tiles in **Media** show a thin bar for how far you have watched.
- **Song progress bar.** The progress bar now shows for music from your library too: drag it to move through the song.
- **Open in your browser.** Surf's new button opens the page in your PC's own browser, for sites that won't work in a built-in browser (such as Google sign-in). Friends in your session still see its window.

## Changed

- A video or a song, not both: starting a video stops music from your library, so only one is playing and highlighted.
- Surf: sign-in buttons (Google, PayPal, Shop…) open their small window instead of spinning, downloads go straight to your Downloads folder, fewer captchas, and a fix for YouTube videos that didn't show until the page was reloaded.
- Surf has a new glass toolbar and new start page tiles.
