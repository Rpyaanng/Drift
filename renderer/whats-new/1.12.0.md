## New

- **Pachinko.** Pick **Pachinko** under **Scenes → Games room** for a tall machine of brass pins. Hold the mouse down on the machine (or hold **Hold to play**) and a steel ball goes up the lane every 0.6 seconds, round the top and down through the pins. The **Power** dial (or the mouse wheel over the machine) sets how hard: softer drops on the right, harder carries round to the left. Each ball costs 25 chips, so it's high stakes: a bad run empties your wallet in a minute or two.
  - The **start pocket** under the window pays 100 and spins the reels. Two alike is a **reach**, and the last reel slows down. Three of a kind is a **fever**: the big gate at the bottom opens for six rounds, and every ball in it pays 500. Two **tulips** pay 110, and open wider after they catch one.
  - Sevens or the cat is a **big fever**, and the next 20 spins are much likelier to hit again.
  - It's a carnival: sunburst rays turning behind the pins, pins that light up as balls hit them, googly eyes over the reels that watch your ball, tulips that bloom, confetti and "+100"s where the pockets pay, and a drain that's a swirling black hole. In a fever the whole machine goes rainbow, shakes, and the gate turns into a big chomping mouth.
  - A **trampoline** slides from side to side across the lower board. Land on it and your ball springs back up into the pins for another go at the pockets.
  - Aim pays off: drop your balls just beside the window and they roll down towards the start pocket.
- **One machine for the whole hub.** At a hub, up to eight of you fire into the same pins, each ball in its owner's colour, and they knock into each other. A fever opens the gate for everyone, so whoever gets balls in it wins, not only whoever hit it. The hub keeps a board of its five biggest fevers.
- **Drift in a window.** **Settings → Show Drift → In a window** puts Drift in one window you can move, resize and keep beside other things, instead of full screen on every monitor. It has its own title bar, which comes and goes with the mouse so the scene stays whole. The bar says what's on and has the controls that matter there: back, pause and next for a scene or your music, or back, forward and reload for a web page. Keep it above other windows, or go back to full screen on every monitor, from the same bar. Drift remembers where you left the window.
- **Your sign-ins come to Surf.** When a site opens in your own browser (for one Surf can't sign in to), the accounts you sign in to there come over to Surf once you close that window, and Surf's pages reload already signed in.

## Changed

- **One wallet for the casino.** Blackjack, Roulette, Texas Hold'em and Pachinko now play from the same chips. Win at the wheel and you have more to bet at Blackjack. Run out anywhere and you're out everywhere for 20 seconds, then back in with 1,000.
  - Alone, your wallet is kept for next time. It starts with the most chips you had at any one table before.
  - At a hub, you start with 1,000 at the first table you sit at, and your chips stay with you from table to table while the hub is running.
- **Game table sounds** now include Pachinko's: the ball's flick up the lane, the pins ticking, a chime for the start pocket, the reels whirring, a rising build for a reach, a fanfare for a fever, and the balls pouring in when it pays.
- **No top limit at Blackjack.** Bet as much as you have, and **All in** puts your whole stack down in one click.
- **One button to raise at Hold'em.** The slider starts at Check (or Call) and each step right raises a little more, up to All in. The button says what it will do.
- **Shares stay on at a hub.** A share used to be kept off while anyone online was away from the sharer's network, which kept people from shares they could actually reach (friends often get in another way, such as a VPN). Now it goes on for everyone, and comes off only if someone says they couldn't connect.

## Fixed

- After a monitor was turned on or off, or Drift went between a window and full screen, Surf's pages could stop answering clicks and keys. They follow the new window now.
- Someone joining Sketch & Guess partway through a game now gets their turn to draw in that round, instead of waiting for the next one.
- Media's music screen shows the song another app is playing (Spotify, a browser...) even while a Drift playlist is paused, not only when none is on.
