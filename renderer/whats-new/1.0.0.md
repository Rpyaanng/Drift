First release of Drift: ambient visuals and soundscapes across all your monitors.

## What's in it

- **Scenes**: shader scenes plus algorithm scenes (Sierpinski zoom, sorting algorithms, Game of Life, maze solver, Julia sets, Hilbert curve, Voronoi), with optional captions
- **Colour**: palette swatches, including a colour-cycle swatch with adjustable speed
- **React to sound**: scenes respond to music, with an optional spectrum or waveform overlay
- **Video**: play your own video folders, mirrored or spanned across monitors; Dolby / DTS audio is converted automatically (ffmpeg included)
- **Music**: your own music folders with search, built-in soundscapes, or control of the Spotify desktop app

Keys: `Tab` controls · `Space` play/pause · `←`/`→` previous/next · `↑`/`↓` volume · `1`–`9` scenes · `P` colour · `C` clock · `B` breathing guide
