## New

- **Roulette.** Pick **Roulette** under **Scenes → Games room** for a European wheel with one zero. Choose a chip and click the layout: on a number, on the line between two for a split, on a corner for four, or on the edge for a street. The outside bets are there too, and everything pays the book's odds, from 35 to 1 down to even money. Right-click takes your last chip back, and **Same again** repeats last spin's bets. The ball goes when everyone has pressed **Spin**, or 25 seconds after the first bet.
  - At a hub, up to eight of you bet on the same layout at once, each in your own colour, with everyone's pointer showing. The hub picks the number, and keeps a board of its five biggest wins.
- **Texas Hold'em.** A no-limit table for up to six, blinds 5 and 10, with side pots when someone goes all in. Fold, check, call, or pick a raise with the slider (½ pot, pot, all in). Your two cards are only ever sent to your PC, and everyone's turn over at a showdown.
  - Alone, you play three computer players. They only know their own cards and the board, like you.
  - At a hub, you play the people there. If you're the only one at the table, a computer player keeps you company until someone else sits down. The hub keeps a board of its five biggest pots.
- **Sketch & Guess.** A drawing game for everyone online in your hub. Each turn one of you picks a word from three and draws it, and the rest type guesses. Guess sooner to score more, and the drawer scores for everyone who gets it. A letter of the word shows at half time and another at three quarters, and a guess one letter off is told it's very close (only you see that). Everyone draws once a round, three rounds a game, up to twelve players.
  - The board is laid out like a diagram editor: a pen, lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, triangles and an eraser down the left (each with a key), and 15 colours, three widths and three fills along the top. Hold Shift for straight lines, squares and circles.
  - Everyone watches the drawing appear as it's drawn. Right guesses are never shown to the others, and once you know the word, your messages only go to the people who know it too.
  - It needs two or more people, so it's only at a hub. The hub keeps a board of its five best scores.
- **The same as Blackjack.** Run out of chips at Roulette or Hold'em and you're back in with 1,000 after 20 seconds. Alone, each table keeps your chips for next time; at a hub, everyone starts fresh. Click someone in the hub bar to sit down beside them, and **Stand up** to watch. None of the games are ever picked by the automatic scene changes.

## Changed

- **Game table sounds.** The switch that was **Blackjack table sounds** in the Hub tab's Sounds now covers every game: the roulette ball rattling round and dropping, a check's knock, a fold, a right guess, a near miss, and time running out.
