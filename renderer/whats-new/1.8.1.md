## New

- **Together's sounds.** Soft chimes when you go online or offline, join or leave voice, mute or unmute, go live, when friends come and go or start broadcasting, and for messages you aren't looking at. Only you hear them, and they're tuned to sit under the Ambient music. Turn them off, set their volume, or try each one in Together → Options → Sounds.

## Fixed

- **Pointers that stop moving now disappear.** In Broadcast, Surf and the pointers drawn on the broadcaster's screen, a pointer that hasn't moved for 6 seconds fades away completely instead of staying faintly on screen, and comes straight back when it moves.
- On the broadcaster's screen, a pointer that had faded no longer reappears whenever someone else moves theirs.
