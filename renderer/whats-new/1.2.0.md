## New

- **Subtitles.** Shown automatically in your language when a video has them. Drift reads tracks stored inside the video, subtitle files next to it (`.srt`, `.vtt`, `.ass`, `.ssa`, also in a `Subs` folder), and any file you load.
- **Find subtitles online.** In the Video tab, **Find online…** fills in the film or show name from the file name and lists matches, closest release first. No account is needed. Only the name in the search box is sent, to Cinemeta and an OpenSubtitles mirror, and only when you press Search.
- **Look and timing.** Size, colour, outline / shadow / box style, height on screen, and timing in 0.1 s or 0.5 s steps. The track and timing you choose are remembered for each video.
- **Six mathematical geometry scenes** (Scenes tab, fourth row): Quasicrystal, Hyperbolic Tiling, Apollonian Gasket, Phyllotaxis, Rose Curves and Chladni Plate, each with a caption explaining it.
- New keys during a video: `S` subtitles on/off, `V` next subtitle track, `Z` / `X` subtitles earlier / later. There is also a CC button on the toolbar.

## Fixed

- Videos with Dolby Digital Plus (E-AC-3) or other Dolby / DTS sound played in silence in copies of Drift built without `ffmpeg.exe`. Drift now also looks for ffmpeg in `vendor/` and on the PATH, and tells you in the Video tab when it is missing.

## Notes

- Picture-based subtitles (Blu-ray / DVD style) are listed but can't be shown.
- The first time a subtitle track inside a large video is used, reading it takes a few seconds.
