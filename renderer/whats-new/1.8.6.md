## New

- **Together is now the Hub tab, and Broadcast is Share.** Same places, clearer names.
- **Sharing your screen, redone.** The Share tab has two columns: your screens and applications on the left, and on the right a large picture of what you picked with **Go live** always in view, however many windows are open. While you're live, Watching, Sound and pointers sit beside the picture and the controls.
- **The Broadcast grid.** Broadcast is now a tab beside Scenes, Media and Surf. It turns that screen into a grid with a tile for every broadcast in your hub, each showing a still until someone watches it. **Watch** fills the screen and **Back** (or Esc) goes back to the grid. **Preview** plays several broadcasts side by side, and your own tile previews what you're sending. A hub can now have 4 broadcasts at once.
- **Emoji, reactions and pictures in chat.** An emoji picker, emoji reactions on any message, and pictures and GIFs: paste, drop or press +, up to 4 MB. Photos are saved again first, so where they were taken isn't sent.
- **Chat from anywhere.** While you're online in a hub, **Enter** opens a small box to write in from anywhere in Drift. Over Surf, Enter still belongs to the page, so use the 💬 in the bottom-left corner.
- **Tabs underline what's on screen.** Scenes, Media, Surf and Broadcast sit in one pill, and a sliding underline marks the one that's showing. Back and Forward grey out when there's nowhere to go.
- The hub's sounds have their own volume setting.

## Fixed

- YouTube in Surf no longer stutters while the cats are on. Each cat is drawn once and then only moved, and the cats sleep while a Surf page makes sound.
- The hub bar stays off Surf pages and broadcasts and comes back next to their own bar. On the Surf stream, it shows who's watching instead of the tab's title.
- If you host the Surf stream, you hear it at your Surf volume, and your friends get it at full volume.
- Back skips a tab's blank start page.
