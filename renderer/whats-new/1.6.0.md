## New

- **A real advert blocker in Surf.** Surf now uses uBlock Origin instead of a simpler blocker. It removes YouTube's video ads and far more of what news sites show, and keeps its filter lists up to date by itself. The first time it's needed, it is downloaded once (about 6 MB) and checked.
- **Copy-protected video plays in Surf** (Widevine), and reaches friends watching with you.
- **Mirror a page onto your other monitors**, and have Surf draw pages on the graphics card if you like.
- **The keyboard shortcuts every browser has** in Surf: Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Shift+T, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+1–9, Ctrl+L / Alt+D, Ctrl+R, zoom with Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0, Alt+Left / Alt+Right, and more.
- **A Halo visualizer overlay**, plus peak caps and a new look for the spectrum and waveform overlays.
- **The clock shows the date**, with the time large and thin and a line that fills across each minute.

## Changed

- Updates: Drift tells you when a new version is out and downloads it when you ask. "Update Drift automatically" starts off for new installs.
- Surf presents itself to sites the way Chrome does, so bot checks ask for fewer captchas.
- Pages you share go out as H.264 on the graphics card when it can encode it, which is lighter on the processor.
- Surf's search box searches Google instead of YouTube.
- The fire sound crackles and pops instead of hissing, and listening to the PC restarts by itself when the sound device changes.
