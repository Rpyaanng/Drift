## New

- **Send files of any size.** Press **+**, paste or drop any file on the chat and press **Enter**. It stays on your PC, and friends download it straight from yours: the hub never holds it. Pictures and GIFs up to 4 MB are kept on the hub as before, so they show while you're away.
- **Every piece is checked.** A file goes in 16 MB pieces, and each one is checked as it arrives against the check that came with the offer. A damaged piece is fetched again, so a changed or broken file is never kept.
- **Downloads carry on by themselves.** If the hub drops or the sender goes offline, a download keeps what it has and carries on once they're back, even after Drift restarts. **Pause** keeps it until you press **Resume**.
- **Big files go directly.** Files up to 500 MB go through the hub's relay, so nobody learns anyone's address. Bigger ones connect your two PCs directly, and Drift asks first because each of you then sees the other's IP address. A direct download keeps going even if the hub drops.
- **What's new, when you update.** The first time a new version opens, this window shows what changed. Find it again, with every past version, in **Settings → Updates → What's new**.

## Improved

- **Know before you share whether friends can reach you.** When you go online in a hub, Drift checks whether friends on other networks could connect to your PC. If they couldn't, **Share to hub** says why before anything starts.
- Downloads go to **Downloads\Drift**. Change the folder in the Hub's **Options**.
- Before you open a program, script or shortcut someone sent you, Drift asks whether you trust it.

> Downloading a file needs both of you on this version.
