## New

- **A paint bucket.** In Sketch & Guess, pick the bucket (or press B) and click to fill an area with your colour, up to the lines around it. Undo and the eraser work on fills like on strokes.
- **Sketch & Guess settings.** The gear at the bottom of the tools sets the time to draw (15 seconds to 10 minutes), how many rounds (1 to 10), whether hints are on, and the word list. Change the words to your own, or press **Reset to Drift's words** to go back. Your settings are used at a hub you host, from the next game; at someone else's hub, the host's are used.
- **Harder words.** Drift's own list has fewer of the very easy words and many more tricky ones: words made of two (*starfish*, *doorbell*), ideas (*gravity*, *nostalgia*), jobs (*ventriloquist*), goings-on (*job interview*, *pillow fight*) and a few sayings (*raining cats and dogs*).
- **Sketch & Guess sounds like a game show.** A drumroll when a game starts, a fanfare for each new round and for your turn, a "lock it in" when the word's picked, a slide whistle for a near miss, a pop when a hint letter shows, the clock ticking through the last ten seconds, a buzzer when time runs out, and at the end a fanfare if you win, applause, or the sad trombone if you came last.
- **The card tables play like a spectator sport.** Chips that clink, stack and slide, a riffle shuffle before the deal, an air horn and a celebration for a big win (blackjack, ten times your bet at roulette, a pot with someone all in), a soft "wah-wahh" when you lose, and a heartbeat under the roulette ball and the river.
- **Winnings roll up.** When chips come in, chip counts and winnings count up to their new total, with a tally ticking along for your own.
- **The Games room tiles are pictures of the tables.** Each game's tile in **Scenes** shows its table mid-game.

## Improved

- **Hints in Sketch & Guess.** Up to three letters show in random spots as the time runs out (at 60%, 40% and 20% left), never giving a short word away, and they can be turned off.
- **Your word, spelled out.** The drawer sees the word on its lines, letter by letter, like the guessers' blanks.
- **The roulette ball** sounds like a ball: rolling round the track, slowing, and clattering over the diamonds before it drops. And applause sounds like real hands.

## Changed

- **Simpler drawing tools.** The line, arrow and shape tools are gone, leaving a pen, the paint bucket and an eraser. Everyone at a hub needs this version to see each other's fills.

## Fixed

- At a hub's Texas Hold'em table, the cards in the middle could be replaced by the hub's list of biggest pots. They show properly now.
