## New

- **Hubs: chat with friends inside Drift.** Host a hub with channels, invites and history, kept on your PC. Friends join with an invite code and can use it while your Drift is open.
- **Together and Chat are one tab.** Share what you're watching from a hub, and friends join from the chat.
- **Voice calls.** Join a call from the Together tab. Drift checks every connection and shows a lock on each one it verified, end-to-end encrypted in calls of up to 8. Push to talk, and an option to connect directly for lower delay.
- **Chat over the video while you watch together.** Press Enter to write without opening the panel.
- **Profile pictures**, and a name friends see.
- **Summon the cat**: a small glowing cat that sits on the panel or the bottom of your screen and wanders between your monitors. Opening or closing the panel under it shoos it off. (In Settings → Extras.)

## Changed

- **A cleaner look for every tab.** Together, Settings, Scenes and Media are laid out in cards. Each setting has a short title with a line on what it does, and its switch or dropdown on the right. The keyboard shortcuts are now a list of keys.
- The switches in Together's options no longer sit on top of their labels.
