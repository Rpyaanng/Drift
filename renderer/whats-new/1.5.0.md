## New

- **Surf: a web browser inside Drift.** Choose the **Surf** tab and a browser opens on that screen, on Drift's own start page: a clock, a search box (an address, or something to search YouTube for) and a few places to drift to. Tabs, back, forward and the address are in a bar that drops down from the top edge. Adverts and trackers are blocked (Settings → *Block adverts in Surf*).
- **Browse together.** While you share from **Together**, friends see and hear the page in front, streamed straight from your PC. With *Friends can play, pause and skip, and use a web page* ticked they can click, scroll and type in it from their own PC too.
- **Everyone's pointer.** Each person's mouse shows over a shared page as a rounded arrow with their name, in their own colour.
- **Listen together.** Friends in your session hear your ambient sound and music from your library, in step with you.
- **Who is where.** During a session each tab in the panel shows how many people are on it.
- **Drift now updates itself.** It checks for a new release now and then, downloads it in the background and installs it when you close Drift. You can also install it straight away, or turn this off, in **Settings → Updates**.

## Changed

- **New icon:** the two waves from Surf's start page.
- Your music now sits next to your videos in the **Media** tab, and ambient sound has moved to **Scenes**.
- The README has a Surf section with screenshots.
