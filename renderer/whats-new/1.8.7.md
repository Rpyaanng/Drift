## New

- **Surf's start page has widgets.** Press **Customize** to choose and arrange Weather, Markets, Headlines, Shortcuts, Notes, This PC and Your browsing, and to set the clock and background.
- **Showing guests your start page is up to you.** If you host a hub, two new settings in Settings decide whether guests see your PC's specs and your browsing on the Surf stream's start page. Both are off until you turn them on. Only sites are shown, never individual pages, and only you can change the hub's start page.

## Fixed

- **Sharing Surf with others feels less awkward.** The page now follows one person's mouse at a time. Clicking or scrolling takes over at once, and anyone else takes over once that person has kept their mouse still for a moment. Before, two people moving at once made menus and links flicker between them. Everyone's pointer still shows.
- **On a friend's Surf stream, your pointer looks like a normal one.** It turns into a hand over links and a text cursor over text boxes. It used to be a fixed arrow with your own name on it.
