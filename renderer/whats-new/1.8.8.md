## New

- **Enter opens the hub chat everywhere.** While you're online in a hub, press **Enter** from any screen to write a message, Surf included.
- **The quick chat box shows the conversation.** Opened while you aren't watching anything of that hub's, it shows the channel's last few messages above it. Your message joins them, and the log stays up for a few seconds after you send, with any replies that come in.
- **Just for you, or for the hub?** Picking a video or music while you're in a hub asks first whether to play it just for you or share it with everyone.

## Fixed

- **A share some friends can't reach stays off the hub's stream.** If someone online couldn't connect to your PC, your video plays just for you instead of leaving them with a blank screen.
- Drift's own "are you sure?" box replaces the plain Windows one, and Surf's start page takes Drift's accent colour.
- Mirrored Surf keeps the Surf monitor's layout, stretched partway toward the shape of your other monitors.
