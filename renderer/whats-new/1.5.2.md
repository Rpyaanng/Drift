## New

- **A new icon.** A two-tone teal and purple swoosh.
- **Back where you left it.** Drift opens with the video or song you had on, paused at the same spot and ready to carry on when you press play.
- **Surf remembers your tabs.** Close Drift with the browser open and your tabs come back next time, on the same monitor, without anything starting to play by itself.

## Changed

- Smoother video next to busy scenes: if heavy scenes on your other monitors start holding a video back, they ease off their frame rate (never their sharpness) until the video plays smoothly again.
- Scenes keep to the frame rate you set on high refresh rate monitors (60 fps on a 144 Hz screen was drawing 72), which saves a little graphics work.
- The soft backdrop beside vertical videos is lighter on the graphics card.
- Converted video sound (Dolby, DTS and the like) now starts in step when a video picks up where it was left.
- The visuals react to a video's own sound when its sound is on.
