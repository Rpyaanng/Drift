## New

- **The crane machine.** Pick **Crane machine** under **Scenes → Games room** for a tall glass box full of cat plushes, with a gift box in the middle of the pile: the grand prize. A try costs 1,000 chips. Press **Play** and the claw sweeps back and forth over the pile by itself. Press **Drop** (or Space, or click the glass) and it comes down, closes, and carries what it caught to the chute.
  - A plush pays 1,000, so winning one gets your try back. A big plush pays 2,000. The grand prize box pays 3,000 and a scene token.
  - It's a game of timing. The claw hangs on a cable, so after you press Drop it slides on a little and swings as it goes down. The swing is the same each time for the same moment in the sweep, so you can learn it. Come down dead centre on a prize while the claw is still and it holds on most of the time. Come down off centre, or still swinging, and it can slip out on the way home.
  - Everything rolls, tumbles and tips. When the box lands at a slant, catch its top corner and the claw hooks it ("HOOKED IT!"), which holds on far more often than an ordinary grab.
  - At a hub there's one seat and a queue. When people are waiting, the seat passes on after each try, or after 25 seconds without a try. Everyone else watches the claw live. The hub keeps a board of its best prizes.
- **Scene tokens and locked scenes.** Scenes are now something you win. Five are open from the start, one at the top of each section: Aurora, Mountain Lake, Sierpiński Zoom, Platonic Solids and Quasicrystal. The rest show a padlock until you unlock them. Every game, and your own videos, are always open.
  - You win a scene token for the crane machine's **grand prize**, for **filling a fever round at Pachinko** (getting the round's balls into the gate before time runs out), and for **winning a game of Sketch & Guess** at a hub.
  - A Pachinko token goes to everyone sitting at the machine, whoever's spin started the fever. A round needs fewer balls per player the more of you are playing: 6 alone, 10 for two, 12 for three, 15 for four or more. Bring friends.
  - Tokens are yours, kept on your own Drift, wherever you win them, even at a friend's hub. Your count shows next to your chips at the crane machine, Pachinko and Sketch & Guess.
  - Everyone starts with one token, so you can try Scene Slots straight away.
- **Scene Slots.** A new machine under **Scenes → Games room**. Put in a token and pull. The machine rocks and jumps, the reels spin, and the last one crawls in. Then the camera zooms into the reels, the screen goes white, and the scene you won appears, live, with its name on a banner. Each scene is marked R, SR or SSR. That's only for show: every locked scene has the same chance. Click to go back to the machine. Your new scene is waiting in the Scenes tab.

## Changed

- **One wallet everywhere.** Your casino chips now go with you. The chips you play at a friend's hub are the same chips you play alone, and whatever you win or lose there stays with you after you leave, at the next hub too. Before, each hub gave you a fresh 1,000 that stayed behind when you left.
- **Smaller stakes.** A new wallet starts with 500 chips, and running out puts you back in with 500 after the 20-second wait (it was 1,000). Chips you already have are untouched.
- **Blackjack has a top bet again: 10,000.** With more than that in front of you, **All in** turns into **Max** and bets 10,000. This is because of a certain player.
- **Sketch & Guess crowns its winners.** At the end of a game the winners' pictures wear a crown, and when the top score is tied, everyone on it wins. Sketch & Guess now sits in its own **Multiplayer only** section of the scene picker, since it needs people.
- **Double-click a hub to go online in it.** When you're offline in a hub, double-click it in the list on the left to go back online. This replaces the **Connect** button. The **⋯** menu now only appears when it has something in it.
- **Pausing no longer hands your play buttons to Spotify.** Before, pausing a video or Drift's music meant back, play and next went to Spotify's song. Now they stay with Drift's own media until you eject it. In the Scenes tab they always control the scene.
- **Game table sounds** now include the crane machine's: the coin going in, the claw dropping and grabbing, a slip, a plush down the chute, and a fanfare for the grand prize. Scene Slots has its own: the lever's ratchet, the reels whirring and clunking to a stop, a heartbeat while the last one turns, and bells and coins for the win.
- At a hub, friends can see when you're at Scene Slots. Clicking you opens their own Scenes tab, since everyone has their own machine.

## Fixed

- Friends' pointers no longer show at the Blackjack and Hold'em tables. Each player sees those tables turned so their own seat is in the middle, so a pointer never landed where it was aimed.
