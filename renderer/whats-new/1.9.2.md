## New

- **Media shows what other apps are playing.** When none of Drift's own music is on, Media's screen shows the song playing in Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music in a browser or any other app that shows up in Windows' volume pop-up, with its cover. You don't need an account or a key.
- **More about the song.** Use the arrows at the edges of Media's screen to look around. On the right is the singer, from Wikipedia (for a band, its lead singer). On the left are the song's numbers from Deezer: how popular it is, the artist's fans, when it came out, its album, genre, length and tempo. A line under the artist says what Wikipedia knows about the song. Links open in Surf.
- **The cover colours the screen.** Media's screen picks up the colours of the song's cover, with a blurred copy of the cover behind it, and an equaliser moves beside **Now playing**.
- **"What's playing" colours.** A new palette that takes its colours from what's on: the page open in Surf, a friend's broadcast you're watching, or the song's cover. Every monitor gets the same colours.
- **A clock in the bar.** The time sits at the right end of the bottom bar, beside the exit button. Hover over it to see the date.

## Improved

- **Surf looks like a real browser.** Tabs show their sites' icons, and a speaker on a tab that's playing sound. The address bar shows the site bright and the rest dimmed, with a padlock or a warning in front. Reload turns into Stop while a page loads, and double-clicking the empty tab strip opens a new tab.
- **Pick a style for Surf's bar.** **Settings → Surf bar style** has Glass, Tinted (your accent colour), Graphite, Daylight, Aurora and Minimal.
- **Choose how a mirrored page is sized.** **Settings → Mirrored page size** picks how Surf fits when it shows on your other monitors too: the Surf monitor's own size, the widest monitor, the sharpest one, or the layout of a monitor you pick (a TV everyone watches, say).
- **Mirror follows you.** In Mirror mode, picking Scenes or Media on one monitor shows it on all of them.
- **Easier on your graphics card.** Scenes stop drawing while a Surf page covers them, and while a video plays in Surf your other screens hold still and the cats nap.
- The app icon is a little larger, so it reads better on the taskbar.
