## New

- **Choose which scenes rotate.** While automatic scene changes are on, every scene tile has a tick in its corner. Untick a scene to leave it out of the rotation; Next and Previous still go through all of them. A line under the tiles says how many scenes are in the rotation.

## Changed

- **Smoother scene changes.** A scene is now built in the background, so the picture keeps moving while the next one gets ready.
- **New README** with screenshots of the scenes, the panel and the Span / Mirror / Mix modes.
