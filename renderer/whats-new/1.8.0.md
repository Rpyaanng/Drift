## New

- **Broadcast.** Stream one of your monitors, or just one application window, to everyone in your hub, with its sound if you like. Viewers watch full-screen on whichever monitor they choose, and can share their mouse pointer: it shows on your real screen, drawn over your game or program, and on every other viewer's copy. Switch source, pause and stop from the Broadcast tab.
- **One voice room per hub.** Being online in a hub puts you in its voice room, listening. There's no call to start or join: press **Talk** to be heard, **Mute** to stop. Up to 16 people, 8 talking at once, end-to-end encrypted. The tray light shows whether your microphone is open, and clicking it turns Talk on or off.
- **The hub's media stream.** While you're online in a hub, playing a video or music in Media puts it on everyone's screen, in step. If someone else's is already on, everyone gets a card asking whether to switch, and it switches if more than half agree.
- **The hub's Surf stream.** Online in a hub, the Surf tab is one browser the whole hub shares, running on the host's PC in a profile of its own. Anyone can open pages, click, scroll, type and change tabs. Downloads wait for the host to allow them, and the host's own network can't be reached from it.
- **Volume mixer.** Click the speaker in the toolbar for a slider for each sound: Drift as a whole, Ambient, Media, Surf's web pages and Spotify.
- **The cats have names.** Hover over a cat to see who it is: Miso, Pebble, Biscuit, Mochi and eight more.
- **Ghost cats.** Asleep while a video plays, the cats fade to faint ghosts, with their sleepy faces still showing.

## Fixed

- **The cats no longer vanish with one monitor on.** With your other monitors turned off, opening or closing the panel made the cats disappear for up to a minute, as if they'd gone to a monitor that was off. Now they stay and just hop to new spots.
- Turning a monitor off moves its cats to one that's on straight away.

## Changed

- Drift doesn't connect to your hubs when it starts: click a hub to be online in it.
- Surf pages you leave stay open behind the scenes, so coming back to Surf shows them again without loading them again.
