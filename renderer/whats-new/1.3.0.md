## New

- **Watch together.** Play a video, open the new **Together** tab and press **Share what I'm watching**. Friends paste your invite code into their Drift and see the same video in step with you: play, pause and seeking stay together. The video goes straight from your PC to theirs, encrypted, with no server in between. Up to eight friends can join.
  - A friend who already has the same file plays their own copy instead of streaming it.
  - **Picture sent between PCs** sends a smaller copy (1080p, 720p or 480p) when a connection can't keep up.
  - Each person picks their own subtitles. You can keep the controls to yourself, and choose whether the video waits for a friend who is still loading.
- **HDR and unusual formats.** HDR videos (HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision) are converted to SDR on screens that aren't showing HDR, so an HDR monitor next to an SDR one each get the right picture. Formats the built-in player can't play (MPEG-2, Xvid, WMV, AVI…) are converted live. **Settings → Video conversion** has the encoder (NVIDIA, Intel, AMD or the processor), the HDR look and the picture size.
- **Six landscape scenes** (Scenes tab, new **Landscapes** row): Mountain Lake, Misty Forest, Desert Dunes, Ocean Sunset, Rolling Hills and Canyon Night. They follow the palette you pick and react to sound like the other scenes.
- **Dark palette.** A dim, near-black colour scheme for a dark room at night, at the end of the Colour row.

## Changed

- **Exit asks first.** `Esc`, the ✕ button and `Alt+F4` now show "Close Drift?". Press `Enter` to close, or `Esc` again to stay. The sleep timer still closes without asking.
- The controls stay readable when a palette's main colour is very dark.

## Notes

- Friends outside your home network: Drift asks your router (UPnP) to open one port (47800) while you share and closes it afterwards. If the router refuses, the Together tab says so; forwarding that port by hand works too. Windows asks once whether to let Drift through its firewall: allow it.
- Everyone watching together needs this version.
- Converting HDR needs an ffmpeg with zscale or libplacebo. The one in this download has both.
