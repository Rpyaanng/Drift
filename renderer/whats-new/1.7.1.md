## Fixed

- **High-resolution videos play smoothly with the cat out.** The cats' breathing, blinking and tail kept every screen redrawing, which could hold back a 4K video's frames until it froze. Now the cats take a nap while a video plays.

## New

- **The cats nap while you watch.** Eyes shut, a little dimmer, and a "z Z Z" in the opposite colour to your theme. They wake up when you pause.
- **Help for friends joining from anywhere.** A Help button in the Together tab, beside Options, walks you through letting friends outside your home network join. The easy way (turning on UPnP) comes first, then forwarding the ports by hand, filled in with your PC's own address and the ports Drift uses, and what to try if it still doesn't work.

## Changed

- Summon the cat no longer has a description under it.
