## New

- **Media has a screen of its own.** Music plays with the song's own cover (from its tags, or a cover.jpg beside it) on a turning record, with what's next and a spectrum. A paused video shows its name. **Eject** puts your media away.
- **Pick your microphone and speakers.** The Hub's **Options** has both, and a **Test** button that lets you hear what others would hear, without sending anything.
- **Strong noise suppression.** Keyboards, fans and traffic are taken out on your own PC (RNNoise), for about 10 ms of delay. **Standard** and **Off** are there too.
- **The hub bar is a rail.** It runs down the left edge of every screen, with everyone online. Click someone's face to jump to what they're looking at.
  - **Hide cursor** keeps your pointer to yourself.
  - Over a paused shared video, everyone sees each other's pointer.

## Changed

- **You start out of voice.** Being online in a hub no longer puts you in its voice room: press **Join voice**.
- The tabs have icons, and the screen tabs (Scenes, Media, Surf, Share) are grouped apart from Hub and Settings.
- The README is rewritten around the new look, with new screenshots.
