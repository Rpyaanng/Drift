Watch together

Play a video, then share it. Friends who paste your invite code into their Drift see the same video, in step with you: play, pause and skipping stay together. The video travels straight from your PC to theirs, encrypted, with no server in between.

Join a friend

Lower the picture if the video keeps stopping to load: the sharing PC then sends a smaller copy. It applies to videos you share and videos you join.

Joining from outside your home asks your router to open one port while you share, and closes it when you stop. Subtitles, sound conversion and HDR conversion all work for friends too, and each person picks their own subtitles.
